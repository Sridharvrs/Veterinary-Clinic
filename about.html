Our Story

It started with
one rescued stray

In 2009, our founder Dr. Maya Hartley patched up a stray tabby on her kitchen table. Word spread, the kitchen became a clinic, and the clinic became a second home for thousands of pets.

Today, Stackly is a full-service veterinary hospital with 14 veterinarians, a fear-free certified nursing team, and a simple promise we've never broken: treat every animal as if it were our own.

— Dr. Maya Hartley, Founder
The heart behind the clinic

Great medicine starts with genuine care.

Stackly was built on a simple belief: veterinary medicine should feel as compassionate as it is clinically excellent.

From the first hello at reception to the final follow-up call, every part of your pet's experience is designed around trust, comfort and clear communication.

Our Mission

To deliver exceptional, compassionate veterinary care that enriches the bond between pets and the people who love them — regardless of a family's budget or a pet's past.

Our Vision

A community where no pet suffers in silence — where modern medicine, fear-free handling and accessible pricing make great care the norm, not the exception.

Our Values

Compassion first. Honest communication. Lifelong learning. And a deep respect for every creature that walks, hops, swims or flies through our doors.

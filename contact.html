Quick answers

Before you ask

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes for urgent cases. For routine care, booking ahead guarantees a shorter wait.

Do you accept pet insurance?

Yes — we accept all major pet insurers and can claim directly with most.

Are you open on holidays?

Routine hours may vary, but our emergency line is staffed every day of the year.

Can I tour the clinic first?

Absolutely — call ahead and we'll happily show you and your pet around.

What payment methods do you take?

All major cards, cash, and interest-free instalment plans on treatments over $300.

How do I get my pet's records?

Log in to the client portal, or request them by email and we'll send them within 24 hours.
